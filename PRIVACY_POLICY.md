# Privacy Policy — Christ Kingdom Connect

**This is a starting template, not legal advice.** Have an actual
lawyer review this before you publish it — privacy law varies by
where your users are (GDPR in the EU, CCPA in California, etc.) and
a template can't account for your specific situation. What it does
do: accurately describe what this app's current codebase actually
collects, so you're not stuck writing this from scratch or guessing.

*Last updated: [DATE]*

## What we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your
email address, a username, and a display name.

**Profile information.** A bio, profile photo, and country, if you
choose to add them.

**Content you post.** Posts, comments, prayer requests, stories
(photos and videos), and messages in livestream chat.

**Usage data.** Who you follow, which posts you like, which groups
and events you join, and which prayer requests you've marked as
prayed for.

**Device information for notifications.** If you allow notifications,
we store a device push token so we can notify you about activity on
your posts and profile.

**Reports.** If you report content or another user, we store the
report, your account, the content or account reported, and the
reason given.

## What we don't collect

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history
outside the app, or financial information. We don't sell your data
to anyone, and we don't use it for advertising — this app doesn't
show ads.

## How we use what we collect

- To operate the core features of the app: your feed, stories,
  events, groups, prayer wall, and livestream.
- To send you notifications about activity relevant to you (likes,
  comments, follows, prayer responses), if you've enabled them.
- To enforce our Community Guidelines — reviewing reported content,
  and suspending or removing accounts that violate them.

## Who can see what

- Your posts, profile, and prayer requests (unless marked anonymous)
  are visible to other signed-in users of the app.
- Anonymous prayer requests hide your identity from other users, but
  our moderation team can still see who posted them, in order to
  respond to reports or abuse.
- Blocking another user prevents them from following you, commenting
  on your content, or seeing content you've chosen to hide from them.

## Data retention and deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by
contacting us at [SUPPORT EMAIL]. When you delete your account, your
posts, comments, and profile are removed; content already reported
or removed by moderators may be retained in our moderation log for
accountability purposes.

## Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age
required by your local law, which may be higher — check this before
launch). We don't knowingly collect data from children under that
age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at
[SUPPORT EMAIL] and we'll remove it.
