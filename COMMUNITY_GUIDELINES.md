# Community Guidelines — Christ Kingdom Connect

Christ Kingdom Connect exists for believers to connect, encourage
one another, and grow in faith together. To keep it a safe and
welcoming space, everyone who uses the app agrees to these
guidelines.

## What's welcome here

Encouragement, testimony, scripture, prayer requests, questions
about faith, and genuine conversation — including respectful
disagreement.

## What's not allowed

- **Harassment or bullying** of any kind, including targeting
  someone repeatedly or encouraging others to do so.
- **Hate speech** — attacking people based on race, ethnicity,
  nationality, religion, disability, gender, or sexual orientation.
- **Sexual or explicit content.**
- **Threats or promotion of violence.**
- **Spam, scams, or impersonating someone else.**
- **Sharing someone's private information without consent.**

## What happens if you break these

Reported content is reviewed by our moderation team. Depending on
severity, we may remove the content, suspend the account, or ban it
permanently. We keep a record of moderation actions for
accountability.

## Reporting something

Every post, comment, prayer request, and profile has a Report
option. If you see something that violates these guidelines, please
report it rather than responding in kind — our team reviews reports
and acts on them.

## Appeals

If you believe content was removed or your account was actioned in
error, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we'll take another look.

*Christ Kingdom Community Church oversees this app's moderation team
and content standards for the platform.*
